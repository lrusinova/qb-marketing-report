📋 To do manually in FigJam

Step 1 — Desaturate the RACI table colors

Select cells by RACI letter (the FigJam table lets you multi-select cells with Shift+click, or just go column by column) and swap each fill for its lighter counterpart. Use FigJam's built-in color picker — these are all standard palette swatches, not custom colors, so they should show up as preset options:

R — Green (bright) → Light green #CDF4D3
A — Blue (bright) → Light blue #C2E5FF
C — Light teal → no change — already muted
I — Light gray → no change — already muted
Conflict cells (red) → Light yellow #FFECBD

When you recolor cells from light-green/light-blue to the new lighter shade, text color can stay dark (#1E1E1E) — it already reads fine on both. Header row (black background) stays as-is — that's structural, not a "data" color, so it doesn't need muting.

On the asterisks: cells like R* or C* currently mark conflicts with a cryptic star. Once those cells are yellow, the star becomes redundant — I'd drop it and just leave the plain letter (R, C, ?) since the yellow fill alone now signals "needs discussion," and the Open Conflicts panel below spells out exactly what's unresolved. Simpler to read at a glance.
