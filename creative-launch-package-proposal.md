# Creative Launch Package Proposal

**Purpose:** A companion to the broader tier launch package work, scoped down to just Creative's own deliverables. This feeds directly into the pre-kickoff strategy conversation Jenny is now part of, and the broader creative-request process update currently in progress — the goal is for Creative to walk into a launch already knowing roughly what asset load to expect by tier, instead of finding out ad hoc as requests land.

This is separate from always-on creative work and one-off requests unrelated to a launch.

**Status:** Draft for review with Jenny and Soumya before anything is finalized.

---

## Methodology

Same source as the original tier package analysis — the 20 launch epics scanned across FF, Pave, Core, and Cross-BU (Jan–Aug 2026) — but this pass isolates only **Creative's own deliverables**: issues logged as *Marketing Creative Request*, plus generic tasks whose summary clearly names a Creative-owned output (video, design asset, social graphic, deck/one-pager refresh, imagery). Content, web builds, paid-campaign setup, and SEO are excluded here — those are covered separately in the full tier package doc.

**Caveats, stated plainly:**
- Tagging is inconsistent across epics — some log every Creative asset as its own *Marketing Creative Request* (AI Form Builder, Connectors & Workflows), others bundle multiple creative outputs into one generic *Task* (Auto-Schedule's "creative assets — social, email, newsletter, website, webinar, login page graphics" is one ticket covering six things), and a few epics don't break Creative's work out as separate issues at all (AAI Photo Insights, QB Intelligence Package). Where an epic shows zero Creative items, that most likely reflects logging habits, not that Creative genuinely did nothing — flagged per launch below.
- Only 3 of the 20 epics carry an explicit tier tag (T1: MKT-11523, MKT-12825; T2: MKT-11521, MKT-11522). The rest are inferred from asset volume and type, same caveat as the original analysis.

## Proposed Tier 1 — Major / Flagship Launch

*Evidence: MKT-12888 (FF AI Form Builder, "Tier 1" per epic description) — 8 distinct Creative Requests; MKT-12740 (Pave Connectors & Workflows, largest dataset, 2 phases) — 8 Creative Requests; MKT-12825 (Core Auto-Schedule, Tier 1 per child issue) — 2 bundled multi-channel creative-asset requests; MKT-10401/10543 (Vibe GA, cross-BU flagship) — 2 Creative Requests.*

At this tier, Creative consistently produces:

| Asset | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Brand/style launch video** | The hero video for the launch — seen as "Brand Style Launch Video" on AI Form Builder |
| **Teaser video** | Pre-launch social teaser, ahead of the main video |
| **How-to / thought-leadership video(s)** | Often more than one, sometimes phased (Phase 1 update + Phase 2 net-new, as seen on Connectors & Workflows) |
| **Social graphics for organic social** | Distinct from the video — static graphic set |
| **Paid social ad creative** | Static images + video + copy, built specifically for paid |
| **Product imagery / landing page hero visuals** | Screenshots/hero imagery for the dedicated landing page |
| **Email / Intercom banner creative** | In-app and email banner graphics |
| **Sales deck update** | Refresh of the main pitch deck to include the new feature |
| **One-pager update** | Refresh of the standard one-pager |

Auto-Schedule's data point is a useful cross-check: rather than 8 separate tickets, it bundled the same scope into two multi-channel requests (Open Beta phase, GA phase) each covering "social, email, newsletter, website, webinar, login page graphics" — same actual workload, logged differently. Either logging pattern is fine; the point is the *scope* is consistent across both data points.

## Proposed Tier 2 — Meaningful / Plan-Affecting Launch

*Evidence: MKT-11522 (FF T2, AI Label Scanner) — deck/one-pager/slide refresh only, no video request logged; MKT-11852 (Pave, Spreadsheet Import & Direct Edit) — 2 video requests; MKT-11521 (FF T2, AI Photo Insights) — no Creative items broken out at all.*

Noticeably leaner and less consistent than Tier 1:

| Asset | Notes |
|---|---|
| **One video** (how-to or feature demo) | Present in one of the three T2 data points, absent in the other two — this looks optional at T2, not standard |
| **Deck / one-pager / customer-facing slide refresh** | A light update to existing collateral, not new production — this is the one asset type that showed up consistently |

Notably absent at this tier: no brand/style launch video, no teaser video, no paid social ad creative, no dedicated landing-page imagery. This tracks with the general T2 pattern (no press release, no webinar, no dedicated paid spend) — T2 leans on existing templates rather than net-new production.

## Proposed Tier 3 — Minor / Incremental Launch

*Evidence: MKT-12795 ("Tier 2 and 3" Pave epic, leanest data point in the whole dataset).*

**Zero Creative items.** The only assets at this tier were a blog publish, one social post, and two emails — no design or video work at all. At T3, Creative isn't part of the launch.

## Recurring special case: Community Platform Brand Kit

Not a launch tier, but a clean, repeatable Creative deliverable worth its own line: both MKT-12469 (Pave) and MKT-12471 (FF) Community Build epics logged an identical Creative ask —

> Hero banner, category/space header images, badge artwork, community logo treatment

— once per BU, as a single *Marketing Creative Request*. Worth templatizing on its own, separate from the launch-tier packages above, since it recurs on a predictable per-BU cadence rather than per-feature-launch.
