Marketing Project Flowcharts — Decision Trees by Project Type

Not a replacement for the RACI ownership map — this shows the branching logic: what kind of project is this, what decisions get made along the way, and which teams get pulled in at each fork. Shapes: pill = start/end, rectangle = team action, diamond = decision point, hexagon (orange) = unresolved ownership question — flagged, not resolved.
Source key: "Launch" and "New Landing Page" flows are built directly from what you described (including the Andy/Brand-Creative/UX example). The other 10 are drafted from the existing RACI matrix and Jira patterns seen this session — treat those as a starting proposal to correct, not settled fact. Each flow below is tagged accordingly.

What's new in this pass: every flow now shows a brief-drafting step and a Jira-creation step near the start, reflecting the actual process (what goes in the brief, when the Jira issue gets made) rather than jumping straight to team hand-offs. Where the RACI matrix documents a genuine, real ownership overlap (not invented), it's called out as an orange flagged hexagon at the exact point it bites — e.g. who actually owns messaging/copy between PMM, Brand, and Content.
